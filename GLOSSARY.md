# Basepoint

Basepoint is a cloud platform that businesses subscribe to one product at a time: point of sale, accounting, HR, CRM, property, e-signatures and an AI assistant.

## Language

### Businesses and access

**Business**:
An organisation that signs up for Basepoint and pays for one or more products.
_Avoid_: tenant, company, client

**Branch**:
One location of a business, such as a restaurant, shop front or office.
_Avoid_: shop, outlet, store

**Company code**:
The short code that identifies a business when its staff sign in.
_Avoid_: tenant code

**Customer**:
A person or organisation that buys from a business.
_Avoid_: client

**Role**:
A named set of permissions given to staff. A role can also limit which of the business's products its holders use.

**Permission**:
The right to see or do one specific thing, such as Edit Main Accounts. Admins hold every permission.

### Products

**Product**:
One of the seven things a business can use on Basepoint: Duka, Pesa, Bandu, Mteja, Dala, E-Sign and Biashara AI.
_Avoid_: module, app, service

**Duka**:
The point-of-sale product: sales, tills, inventory, recipes, restaurant tables, kitchen display and offline orders.
_Avoid_: POS, retail

**Pesa**:
The accounting product: bookkeeping, invoices, expenses, bank reconciliation, VAT reports and eTIMS.
_Avoid_: accounting, finance

**Bandu**:
The HR product: staff records, shifts, attendance and payroll.
_Avoid_: HR, payroll

**Mteja**:
The CRM product: customer records, leads, campaigns, forms and messaging channels.
_Avoid_: CRM, omnichannel

**Dala**:
The property product: properties, units, leases, rent invoicing and meter readings.
_Avoid_: real estate, property management

**E-Sign**:
The product for document templates and electronic signatures.
_Avoid_: ESign, signature, Document Center

**Biashara AI**:
The AI assistant that answers questions about a business's own data. It appears inside Duka and is not billed on its own.

**Base One**:
Basepoint's mobile app on Google Play, which replaces the separate Owner and Waiter apps.
_Avoid_: Owner App, Waiter App

### Billing

**Plan**:
A price list for one product, charged monthly or yearly. An enterprise plan is a private plan made for a single business.

**Subscription**:
A business's agreement to pay for one product on one plan. A business has one subscription for each billable product it uses.

**Billing cycle**:
How often a subscription is charged: monthly or yearly.
_Avoid_: quarterly

**Price lock**:
The rule that a subscription keeps the price it started on, so changing a plan's price never reprices existing subscriptions without warning.

**Subscription invoice**:
Basepoint's invoice to a business for one billing cycle, with one line per subscription.
_Avoid_: invoice (on its own, an invoice is the business's invoice to its customer)

### Pesa

**Invoice**:
A business's request for payment from one of its customers.

**Main account**:
A top-level account in a business's chart of accounts. Only staff with the Edit Main Accounts permission can edit one, and it can never be deactivated or deleted.

**Transaction lock**:
A date on or before which a business's records in the covered products can no longer be changed. Locks cover whole days.

**eTIMS**:
The Kenya Revenue Authority's electronic tax invoice system, to which Pesa submits a business's invoices.
_Avoid_: ETR

**VAT pricing mode**:
Whether a branch's prices already include VAT (inclusive) or have VAT added on top (exclusive).

### Dala

**Tenant**:
A person or organisation renting a unit in Dala. Never the business that uses Basepoint.
_Avoid_: renter, lessee

**Property**:
A building or estate that a business manages in Dala.

**Unit**:
A space within a property that can be let or sold.

**Lease**:
An agreement letting a unit to a tenant for a period.
